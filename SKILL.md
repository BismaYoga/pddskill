# Pro Graphic Designer (Modern Studio Edition)

Design contemporary, high-taste visual communications that look art-directed by a senior human designer at a modern creative studio—never like generic AI templates or web-developer wireframes.

---

## 1. Anti-AI-Slop Red Lines (Zero Tolerance)

Every element on the canvas must serve visual communication or spatial tension. Strictly ban these amateur AI clichés:

- **NO Web-UI / Div-Box Posters:** Never build a single poster or Slide 1 cover by stacking bordered `.card` boxes, 3 feature cards, dashboard widgets, or neo-brutalist spec tables. Social media graphics must read instantly at mobile scale, not look like a desktop SaaS wireframe.
- **TOTAL BAN on Bullets, Dots & Status Pills:**
  - Never prepend kickers, tags, or headers with `•`, `●`, or pulsing status dots (`● LIVE`, `● ALERT`).
  - Never wrap Slide 1 category kickers in rounded pill borders/capsules (`[ CATEGORY ]`). Pure tracked typography conveys far greater authority.
  - Never use `•` as a metadata separator in footers. Use clean whitespace alignment, a delicate hairline rule, or minimal `//` / `—` only when structurally needed.
- **NO Fake Clickable Buttons:** Never draw website CTA buttons (`[ DAFTAR SEKARANG ]`, `[ APPLY NOW → ]`) on static graphics. For carousels, use a quiet typographic cue (`Geser →`).
- **NO Floating Clipart Satellites:** Never scatter random 3D coins, shields, bells, or floating social icon bubbles around the hero subject.
- **NO Stiff ID/Passport Portraits in News:** Never use blank, static bureaucratic pasfoto for news, policy, or crisis stories. Always use **contextual action photography** (passionate podium gestures, field inspections, authentic motion).
- **NO Hard-Edge Cutouts on Food ("Sticker Effect"):** Never isolate food onto a dark/sterile void. Food relies on macro full-bleed sensory context (natural glaze, steam, wood/stone/linen surfaces, warm ambient light).

## 2. Spatial Architecture & Optical Balance

### 2.1 The Spatial Yield Rule (Subject vs. Typography)
Hero imagery and display typography must **yield space to each other**—never freeze a subject at the dead `50%` vertical center and cram text above or below it:

1. **Top-Anchored Typography (`zone: bottom` asset):**
   - Typography occupies the upper breathing zone (`Y: 95px–480px`).
   - Hero asset yields downward into the lower half (`Y: 520px–1350px`).
2. **Bottom-Anchored Typography (`zone: top` asset):**
   - Hero asset commands the upper 60% (`Y: 60px–780px`) as an immediate visual magnet.
   - Headline lockup grounds the lower third (`Y: 830px–1260px`) over a smooth gradient scrim.
3. **Asymmetric Split Axis:**
   - Hero mass occupies 60%–65% on one side, counterweighted by a tight vertical editorial lockup on the opposite axis.
4. **Pure Typographic Canvas (No Hero Asset):**
   - Bias the core typographic mass to the **Optical Center (`42%–45%` from top)** rather than mathematical `50%` so it never feels droopy.

### 2.2 Optical Equilibrium (Anti-Lopsided Rule)
- **Never** pair a horizontally centered hero asset with a massive left-aligned top headline while leaving the top-right corner empty—minimalist canvases without filler cards will visibly tip left-heavy.
- When the hero asset is centered horizontally, either align the typographic lockup along the **Symmetrical Central Axis** (`text-align: center`) OR shift to a **Bottom-Anchored** full-width editorial lockup.

### 2.3 Safe Margins & Gestalt Proximity
- **Safe Margins:** Enforce `72px–88px` outer padding on a `1080px` canvas. Never clip or crowd letters near canvas edges.
- **Gestalt Lockups:** Never distribute elements evenly (`space-between` across 4+ blocks). Group related thoughts tightly (`10px–16px` gap between kicker, headline, and sub-hook) and leave **35%–50% intentional negative space** separating major zones.

---


### 2.4 The Quadrant Equilibrium Rule (Anti-Jompang / Anti-Lopsided)
Divide the canvas into 4 visual quadrants (Top-Left, Top-Right, Bottom-Left, Bottom-Right):
- **Never leave an entire quadrant as a hollow desert** while the opposite quadrant is overloaded with visual mass.
- If a directional subject (e.g., cybernetic robot arm) enters from Bottom-Left pointing toward Top-Right, the Top-Left must hold the headline lockup, and Bottom-Right must hold the grounding copy container to complete the diagonal kinetic counterweight.
- **Bottom Sheet Containment Ratio:** The height of any bottom sheet or container card must tightly hug its typography (`padding: 48px–56px`). Never leave a 150px–200px blank empty void at the bottom of a card—this creates a hollow "jompang" bottom.

### 2.5 Negative Space & Asset Spatial Harmony (Anti-Crowding & Anti-Occlusion)
Balance is NOT just text alignment—it is the harmonious relationship between **Asset Mass**, **Text Mass**, and **Negative Space (Empty Space)**:
- **Negative Space Equilibrium:** Empty space must be distributed intentionally, not accidentally. Never trap 60% of the canvas as a dead empty void at the top while cramming text and asset into the bottom 40%. The hero asset and typography should occupy the canvas with balanced optical breathing room on all sides.
- **Asset Integrity & Non-Occlusion:** Never awkwardly cover, bury, or clip critical 3D assets behind cards or containers. If a 3D asset overlaps a bottom sheet, the asset must be 80%+ visible and elevated above the card with directional drop shadows, NOT suffocated or amputated by the container.
- **Clean Canvas & Zero Corner Clutter:** Eliminate useless decorative corner tags (e.g. `REPORT // 2026`, fake tech badges, corner checkmarks, arbitrary labels). Modern high-taste design is radically clean: keep only essential anchors (e.g., quiet slide counter `01 / 05` and swipe cue `Geser →`). Let the reader focus 100% on the core message and visual hero.

### 3.1 Modern Social Editorial Copywriting & The "Scroll-Stopper" Hook
A graphic design poster or carousel lives and dies by its editorial hook. Never write like an academic research paper or stiff corporate documentation:
- **Embody the Modern Reader:** Write from the perspective of an ambitious, curious modern professional/creator scrolling their feed. The hook must trigger immediate curiosity, FOMO, or a paradigm shift in under 2 seconds.
- **Tension-Driven Hooks:** Use contrast and provocation over bland descriptions:
  - ❌ *Boring/Academic:* "Analisis Fitur Extended Reasoning Pada Model Claude 3.7 Sonnet"
  - ✅ *Punchy & Relatable:* "Claude AI Itu Bukan ChatGPT. Ini Alasan Kenapa Banyak yang Mulai Pindah."
- **Text Economy (Concise & High-Yield):**
  - Never force paragraphs where space is tight. Say more with fewer, sharper words.
  - Break complex concepts into punchy, conversational, jargon-free explanations that anyone understands immediately.

### 5.1 Dynamic Carousel Pacing Wave (Anti-Monotony Directive)
**Strictly FORBIDDEN:** Repeating the exact same layout template (e.g. identical bottom sheet card) across 3+ consecutive slides in a carousel. Social media audiences disengage immediately from visual repetition.
A high-converting 5-slide carousel MUST follow a dynamic **visual pacing wave**:
1. **Slide 1 (The Hook):** Centered monumental hero asset + punchy 3-line headline + zero paragraph clutter.
2. **Slide 2 (The Paradigm Shift / Two-Tone Split):** Dark top (60%) + Ice-blue curved bottom sheet (40%) with overlapping 3D asset casting deep directional shadow.
3. **Slide 3 (The Mechanics / Isometric Split):** Isometric 3D asset floating in upper field with dual-column or stepped feature highlights below.
4. **Slide 4 (Action / Diagonal Kinetic Vector):** Directional 3D asset (e.g., hand interacting with screen) with cross-diagonal text placement for maximum kinetic energy.
5. **Slide 5 (The Grand Climax / Zero-Sheet Release):** Break out of the bottom-sheet container! Full-bleed deep studio canvas featuring an iconic symbolic 3D sculpture, a masterclass editorial quote, and a clear, quiet bookmark/share takeaway.

## 3. Modern Typography & Mobile Legibility Rules

- **Maximum 3 Hierarchy Tiers:** Limit any single canvas to (1) Micro Kicker/Meta, (2) Hero Display Headline, and (3) Editorial Sub-hook.
- **Tight Tracking Requires Word-Spacing Compensation:**
  - Modern heavy display headlines (`72px–104px`, weight `800–900`) look sharp with tight negative tracking (`letter-spacing: -2px` to `-3px` / `-0.03em`).
  - **MANDATORY:** Whenever `letter-spacing <= -1.5px`, explicitly add **`word-spacing: 8px` to `12px` (`0.08em–0.12em`)** so adjacent uppercase words (`WEBSITE KAMU`) never collide into `WEBSITEKAMU`.
- **Mobile Legibility Floor (`18px` Minimum on `1080px` Canvas):**
  - A `1080px` post shrinks ~3× on phone screens (`360px` CSS width).
  - **NEVER set any text smaller than `18px`** (`12px–14px` becomes unreadable `4px` dust on mobile).
  - Recommended `1080×1350` scale:
    - **Hero Headline:** `72px–104px`, `line-height: 0.95–1.02`
    - **Sub-hook / Editorial Serif:** `30px–42px`, `line-height: 1.22–1.32`
    - **Kicker / Counter / Swipe Cue:** `18px–22px`, `letter-spacing: 0.16em–0.22em`
- **Genre-Matched Font Personas:** Select contemporary Google Font pairings and color systems from [agency-design-archetypes.md](./references/agency-design-archetypes.md). Never put aggressive gym/condensed fonts (*Bebas Neue*, *Anton*) on food, luxury hospitality, or clinical healthcare.

## 4. Seamless Asset Blending & Contrast Gate

- **Zero Bounding-Box Seams (Anti-Pasted-Rectangle):**
  - Never paste a square render/photo onto a dark or colored canvas if its background RGB (`#0b0d13`) differs even slightly from the canvas hex (`#050609`)—OLED screens expose faint rectangular borders immediately.
  - Always blend standalone studio assets onto a unified `1080×1350` canvas using [`prepare_asset.py`](./scripts/prepare_asset.py) (`place-seamless` mode), which clamps background black levels to match the exact canvas hex and applies a smooth elliptical radial feather to zero before the asset edges.
- **WCAG 4.5:1 Scrim Protection:**
  - Never place light or thin typography directly over bright sky, sunlit leaves, mist, or busy highlights. Always apply a multi-stop CSS linear/radial scrim behind text zones to guarantee effortless legibility.

## 5. Instagram Carousel Architecture

- **Slide 1 (Thumb-Stopping Hook Cover):**
  - **Under 15 words total** across the entire canvas: Clean Kicker + High-Tension Hook Headline + 1-line Provocative Sub-hook + Quiet Slide Counter (`01 / 05`) & Swipe Cue (`Geser →`).
  - Zero paragraphs, zero step lists, zero data boxes on Slide 1.
- **Slides 2 to N-1 (Editorial Value Breakdown):**
  - Use clean Swiss architectural grids: `1px` subtle hairline dividers (`rgba(255,255,255,0.10)` or `rgba(17,18,21,0.12)`), oversized typographic index numbers (`01`, `02`), consistent `80px` alignment grid, maximum 40 words per slide.

## 6. Execution Workflow (Low-RAM Safe & Self-Verified)

Follow these 5 steps strictly on every design task:

### Step 1: Memory-Safe Asset Preparation (1GB RAM Guard)
- **NEVER** open raw master images (`>2500px` or `>10MB`) directly with raw `Image.open()`—this triggers instant Linux OOM crashes (`exit 137`). When downloading from Wikimedia/web APIs, always fetch `1280px–1600px` thumbnails.
- Use [`prepare_asset.py`](./scripts/prepare_asset.py) for memory-safe JPEG draft decoding and seamless canvas placement:
  - Full-bleed cover crop:
    `python3 scripts/prepare_asset.py cover <input_img> <output.jpg> --width 1080 --height 1350 --focus top`
  - Seamless dark/solid studio placement (solves bounding-box seams & Spatial Yield):
    `python3 scripts/prepare_asset.py place-seamless <asset_img> <output.jpg> --zone bottom --bg "#050609"`

### Step 2: Compose Modern HTML/CSS Canvas
- Consult [agency-design-archetypes.md](./references/agency-design-archetypes.md) to pick an archetype suited to the topic's emotional psychology (never reuse the exact same skeleton across unrelated niches).
- Lock `html, body` to exact dimensions (`1080px × 1350px` for 4:5 portrait, `1080px × 1080px` for 1:1, `1080px × 1920px` for 9:16) with `overflow: hidden`.

### Step 3: Render Pixel-Perfect PNG via Helper Script
- Run the low-memory renderer [`render_graphic.py`](./scripts/render_graphic.py) (do NOT write ad-hoc Playwright scripts from scratch):
  `python3 scripts/render_graphic.py <input.html> <output.png> 1080 1350 --preview`

### Step 4: Export True Figma-Editable SVG
- Create a standalone `.svg` (`viewBox="0 0 1080 1350"`) alongside the PNG.
- **Figma Compatibility Rule:** Figma ignores external `@import url(...)` inside `<style>`. Always set `font-family`, `font-weight`, `font-size`, `letter-spacing`, and `fill` **directly as XML attributes** on `<text>` and `<tspan>` elements (see template in [agency-design-archetypes.md](./references/agency-design-archetypes.md)).

### Step 5: MANDATORY Visual Self-Critique Gate (Before Replying!)
- **NEVER send a design to the user blind.** After rendering, you **MUST view the generated PNG/preview image** (`read_file` / `view_file`) and rigorously audit the design against these 9 fundamental criteria:
  1. **Zero Slop:** No `•` bullets/dots, no pill capsules, no fake buttons, no floating clipart, no UI window titlebars?
  2. **Word Spacing & Margins:** No collided uppercase words (`WEBSITEKAMU`) and `>= 72px` safe edge margins?
  3. **Seamless Blending:** Zero visible rectangular seam/box around the hero subject?
  4. **Negative Space Balance:** Is empty space intentionally distributed across the whole canvas, or is there an awkward, lopsided void (e.g. 60% empty top vs crowded bottom)?
  5. **Asset Visibility & Integrity:** Is the 3D hero asset fully celebrated, or is it awkwardly covered, cropped, or suffocated behind containers?
  6. **Reader Empathy & Hook Strength:** Would a modern human stop scrolling? Is the headline punchy, conversational, and compelling—or does it sound like a stiff academic textbook or robot translation?
  7. **Clean Canvas (Zero Corner Clutter):** Are the corners free of unnecessary metadata noise (fake report tags, corner checkmarks)? Is the focus 100% on what matters?
  8. **Text Economy & Pacing:** Is the copy concise, high-yield, and easy to digest without forcing text into tight spaces?
  9. **Mobile Contrast & Scale:** All copy `>= 18px` and crisp (`>= 4.5:1` contrast)?
- If **any** check fails, you must revise the HTML/CSS layout or asset placement and re-render until the graphic looks truly publication-grade.
