# Pro Graphic Designer

Create visual graphic designs that look intentionally crafted by a seasoned human graphic designer, not generated by default-looking AI templates.

## Core Design Principles

### 1. The Anti-AI-Slop Blacklist (Strictly Prohibited)
Never generate graphics that scream "cheap AI template" or "web dev UI wireframe". The following clichés are strictly banned:
- 🚫 **BANNED: The Web-Component / Div-Box Syndrome.** NEVER design a poster by stacking bordered cards (`.data-card`, `.info-card`), 3 neo-brutalist data boxes with hard shadows, or 3 checklist cards. This makes graphics look like Figma website wireframes or boring PowerPoint slides, NOT poster art.
- 🚫 **BANNED: Trapping Photography in Small Cards.** Never shrink an epic landscape or powerful athletic photo into a small bordered card. Let photography command the canvas (full-bleed, cinematic scrims, or bold asymmetric integration).
- 🚫 **BANNED: Mechanical Template Swapping.** Never apply the same visual skeleton (header + left text + right photo box + footer) across diverse genres.
- 🚫 **BANNED: Clipped Typography.** NEVER let headline or body words overflow or touch canvas edges. Always maintain a strict 70px–80px safe margin. Long words (>6 letters) must scale down proportionally.
- 🚫 **BANNED: Jagged / Unrefined Cutouts.** Never use raw, frayed, or jagged threshold cutouts.
- 🚫 **BANNED: Fake Clickable Buttons on Social Media Posts.** NEVER place website-like buttons (`[ APPLY NOW → ]`, `[ DAFTAR SEKARANG ]`) on static feed graphics or posters.
- 🚫 **BANNED: Rogue Asset Colors.** NEVER use assets whose primary colors clash with the canvas palette.
- 🚫 **BANNED: Fake Telemetry & Status Pills.** NEVER place rounded pill boxes containing `● LIVE`, `● SYSTEM OPERATIONAL`, or pulsing green dots.
- 🚫 **BANNED: Random Floating Satellites.** NEVER spray 3–5 unrelated floating icons (coins, bells, shields, trophies) around a hero subject.
- 🚫 **BANNED: Over-explanation.** Avoid dense paragraphs. Let the asset's posture and bold typography speak.

### 2. Radical Design Versatility (Anti-Monotonous Templating)
Never reuse the same wireframe layout across different genres. Tailor the structural architecture, typography pairing, and visual medium to the specific topic:
1. **Sport / High-Energy (Brutalist Street Poster):**
   - *Composition:* Full-canvas kinetic tension, raw barcode graphics, angled tape badges, 3-column brutalist data boxes, registration crosshairs.
   - *Typography:* Ultra-condensed industrial display sans (*Anton* / *Bebas Neue*) paired with monospace data tables.
   - *Palette:* High-contrast light paper (`#f1ede4`), carbon black, and blaze orange / volt lime.
2. **Travel / Luxury Leisure (Editorial Magazine Cover):**
   - *Composition:* Vast serene negative space, fine hairline border frame, asymmetric layered photo collage (large panoramic viewpoint + floating polaroid inset), classical centered narrative prose.
   - *Typography:* Luxury high-contrast serif italic (*Cormorant Garamond* / *Instrument Serif*) paired with widely tracked minimalist sans.
   - *Palette:* Warm sand linen (`#f8f6f0`), deep espresso, and warm terracotta ochre.
3. **Health / Public Wellness (Modular Clinical Infographic):**
   - *Composition:* Clean educational card layout, circular healthcare professional portrait with verified badge, 3 numbered checklist cards (01, 02, 03), official helpline footer banner.
   - *Typography:* Friendly, approachable modern geometric sans (*Plus Jakarta Sans*) with rounded badges.
   - *Palette:* Clean medical sky-blue (`#f0f7fd`), pure white cards, and mint emerald.

### 3. Purposeful Graphic Accents (Balancing Negative Space)
Negative space should breathe, but it should never look abandoned. Instead of filling space with text paragraphs:
- **Use Disciplined Graphic Accents:**
  - Minimalist circular typographic seals (`EST. 2026 • CREATIVE FELLOWSHIP`).
  - Architectural crosshairs (`+`), corner registration marks, and delicate coordinate rules (`01 // MOMENTUM`).
  - Subtle serial numbers / barcode reference tags (`REF: 902-MK-MV`).
- **Hero + Complementary Accent Dynamic:**
  - Maintain **1 primary hero focal point** (80% visual mass).
  - Secondary visual elements (a subtle geometric prism, a floating holographic ring, or a clean graphic stamp) act as visual echoes, never competing with the hero.

### 4. Gestalt Proximity & Typographic Lockup
- **Never space elements out evenly across the canvas.** Even spacing makes graphics look amateur, floaty, and disconnected.
- **Group related thoughts together:**
  - Headline + Primary Tagline = Single tight unit (gap: 8–16px).
  - Category Badge + Headline = Close vertical proximity (gap: 12–20px).
- **Separate sections generously:**
  - Large intentional negative space (48–80px) between the main typographic unit and secondary metadata or CTA.

### 5. Post-Mortem Design Pitfalls & Red Lines (Learned from Real Reviews)
- ⚠️ **The "Website Wireframe" Fallacy:**
  - Never design a social media post (1080x1080 / 1080x1350) like a desktop SaaS landing page.
  - Squeezing 3 columns of 13px text paragraphs under a small photo box is a catastrophic mobile failure. On phones, feed graphics must be bold, instant, and visceral.
- ⚠️ **The "Imprisoned Photo" Trap:**
  - In genres driven by visual emotion (Travel, Nature, Luxury, Sports), never imprison the hero image in a tiny 40% box while filling 60% of the canvas with text and borders.
  - The photography must breathe and dominate (70–100% canvas coverage). Integrate elegant typography directly into the natural negative space of the photograph (sky, sea, misty horizons, shadows) using soft cinematic scrims.
- ⚠️ **The "Stiff Studio Portrait in News" Fallacy (Lack of Contextual Journalism):**
  - NEVER use a stiff, formal studio pasfoto (neutral expression, staring blankly at camera) for news graphics regarding bold actions, policies, or national crises (e.g. food security, protests, economic reforms). It looks like a civil servant identity card or obituary notice, completely draining the news of urgency and drama.
  - **The Action Journalism Rule:** Always pair the news story with **contextual action photography**:
    - *Policy / Declaration:* The leader speaking passionately at the podium with expressive hand gestures, intense eye contact, microphones, and podium gravitas.
    - *Field / Ground Operations:* The subject on-site (walking through rice fields, inspecting harvests, interacting with workers, on military vessels).
  - **Dynamic News Typography:** Avoid polite, timid textbook sentences. News typography demands **bold urgency, high contrast, and dynamic emphasis**: massive heavyweight display sans (*Plus Jakarta Sans 800*, *Syne*, *Cabinet Grotesk*) with highlighted critical phrases in national red or yellow amber, pulling the reader's eye immediately.
- ⚠️ **The "Agency-Grade Editorial Taste" Blueprint (High-Taste Carousel & Slide 1 Hook Mastery):**
  - **Core Pillars of High-Performing Visual Design & Carousel Engagement:**
    1. *The Slide 1 "Pattern Interrupt" Hook:* Slide 1 is NEVER a passive title card. It uses a high-voltage psychological hook:
       - Contrarian stance (*"Everyone is hyping up tool X, but here is why you shouldn't jump in blindly"*).
       - Visual aesthetic eye candy (*soft mesh gradient swatch cards, curated design system tokens*).
       - Culturally relatable industry pain points framed with high editorial elegance (*creative agency deadline realities on pristine minimalist paper*).
       - Real-world experiential case studies (*event activation photobooth funnels, physical-to-digital journeys*).
    2. *Swiss Minimalist Cleanliness & Breathability:* Whitespace is king. Uses warm off-whites (`#f8f8f8`, `#eaeaea`) or deep midnight navy (`#020b18`). Zero clutter, generous margins (>80px), crisp micro-metadata tags.
    3. *Editorial Product/Funnel Visuals:* Visualizes workflows (step 1 to 5 user journey) or actionable color code swatches, making every carousel slide inherently save-worthy and share-worthy.
    4. *Conversational Authority:* Typography balances sharp modern geometric sans (*Plus Jakarta Sans*, *Inter*) with conversational, approachable copy.
- ⚠️ **The "Header-Headline-Photo-Footer Sandwich" Monotony:**
  - NEVER fall into the repetitive trap of always using the same vertical stack:
    1. Top thin header bar with logo + date/edition + horizontal divider.
    2. Large headline at the top.
    3. Photo in the middle.
    4. Bottom horizontal divider + tag line + price/CTA pill.
  - This is a predictable, robotic layout that makes every poster look like a twin of the last one.
  - **Embrace Radical Compositional Diversity:**
    - *Archetype 1: Bottom-Heavy Cinematic Anchor.* Let the top 60% be pure visual spectacle and breathing room (smoke, light, subject). Settle all typography into a confident, ultra-tight editorial lockup at the bottom (like high-end cinema or luxury fashion posters). NO top header bar!
    - *Archetype 2: The Asymmetric Side Column.* Push the subject to the right (or left) 65% and anchor a bold vertical typographic column on the opposite side.
    - *Archetype 3: The Interlocking Title Wrap.* Massive typography sitting across the center or upper third, with the subject physically overlapping the letters, and metadata tucked quietly into one corner.
    - *Archetype 4: The Minimalist Quiet Corner.* 85% photo dominance with minimal, powerful typography tucked into a single intentional corner with massive negative space.
    - *Archetype 5: Vertical Spine & Cross-Axis.* Running vertical text along one margin (`writing-mode: vertical-rl`) contrasted against a horizontal focal headline.
- ⚠️ **The "Gimmick Transplant" Syndrome (Applying Sport/Tech Tricks to Food/Lifestyle):**
  - NEVER copy-paste stylistic gimmicks from one genre into another. Pointer lines with dots (`———●`), HUD coordinates, telemetry badges, and giant angled stencil words belong to industrial sports and tech gear. Applying them to food makes a gourmet dish look like a cold engineering blueprint or medical device.
- ⚠️ **The "Athletic Font on Food" Trap (Font Persona Mismatch):**
  - Fonts have deep psychological personas. Ultra-condensed, tall, rigid sans-serifs (*Bebas Neue*, *Anton*, *Teko*, *Oswald Condensed*) carry an industrial, aggressive, high-adrenaline "sports/gym/streetwear" DNA. Putting *Bebas Neue* on a burger makes it look like an athletic supplement or Nike poster!
  - **Food Typography Rules:** Food is organic, warm, melting, and comforting. Food requires fonts with **fleshy volume, soft rounded curves, vintage diner warmth, or voluptuous bistro serifs**:
    - *Retro Diner & Craft Burgers:* Chunky warm display with soft serifs or curves (*Fraunces 900*, *Shrikhand*, *Cooper Black* style, *Abril Fatface*, *Bricolage Grotesque*, *Recoleta* vibe).
    - *Artisanal Bakery & Wine:* Warm organic serifs (*DM Serif Display*, *Playfair Display*, *Instrument Serif*).
    - *Modern Fast Casual:* Friendly, plump geometric sans (*Plus Jakarta Sans*, *Outfit* with rounded terminals) paired with expressive organic scripts.
- ⚠️ **The "Sticker Effect" in Food Design (Killing Appetite Appeal):**
  - NEVER isolate food by cutting it out with hard edges onto an artificial dark/void gradient. Food advertising relies entirely on **sensory indulgence, texture, and organic context**: melting butter/cheese, crumbs, sizzling cast iron, grease glazes, butcher paper, rustic wooden boards, and warm ambient sunlight/kitchen glow. Cutting food out makes it look like a plastic toy sticker floating in a void.
- ⚠️ **Template Re-Skinning Reflex:**
  - When asked for a new subject, do NOT reach for the skeleton of the previous design and swap words/colors. Start from the **emotional psychology** of the audience for that exact niche.
- ⚠️ **Visual Clutter & Micro-Text Overkill:**
  - Maximum 3 typographic hierarchical tiers. If a poster has a header, eyebrow, badge, giant title, script accent, subhead, 3-column specs, price tag, sub-price, and CTA pill—it is over-engineered AI slop. Strip away 50% of the text; let the subject breathe.

### 2. Optical Center vs Mathematical Center
- On vertical social media canvases (4:5 `1080x1350` or 9:16 `1080x1920`), the human eye perceives the visual center at **42%–45% from the top**, NOT at 50%.
- Positioning the main focal text at 50% makes the composition feel heavy, droopy, and bottom-heavy. Always bias primary typography slightly upwards.

### 3. Bold Scale & Hierarchy Contrast
- One unmistakable **Focal Point** (The Hero Headline).
  - Headline should be bold, confident, and large (72px–96px on a 1080px canvas).
  - Use tight tracking / kerning (`letter-spacing: -2px` to `-3px`) on display sans-serifs.
- Secondary elements (subhead, description) should be clearly subordinated (font size 24px–32px, lighter weight).
- Tertiary metadata (dates, prizes, sponsors) should be compact, disciplined, and quiet (font size 18px–20px).

### 4. Vector Background Artistry (SVG)
- Use layered SVG curves with mathematical Bézier paths (`path d="M... C..."`).
- Combine deep radial gradients with specular highlight strokes (`stroke="url(#gradient)" stroke-width="3-5"`).
- Add subtle glints or lighting flares (✦) along the curve spines for a high-end 3D metallic or glass finish.

- **Archetype B (Web3 SaaS & 3D Glass Security):** Midnight navy, electric cyan, glossy 3D tactile assets (padlocks, shields, coins), rounded status badges.
- **Archetype C (Fintech & Trading Precision):** Obsidian carbon, emerald green neon (`#10b981`), glowing 3D candlestick charts, performance metric chips.
- **Archetype D (Creative Studio Royal Grid):** Electric ultramarine blue, clean white floating cards, rim-lit cutouts, 3D app icons.
- **Archetype E (Clinical Trust & Medical Health):** Pristine white and medical sky blue, structured symptom cards, clear doctor imagery, QR code badge.
- **Archetype F (Campus & Youth Dynamic Collage):** Vibrant cyan, massive stencil typography with human cutout overlapping foreground, halftone and diagonal momentum.

### 6. Dual-Output Deliverable
Always provide two formats for the user:
1. **Production-Ready PNG:** Rendered via headless Chromium at exact social media dimensions (1080x1350 for IG portrait, 1080x1080 for square).
2. **Editable Vector SVG:** Standalone `.svg` file that the user can drag & drop directly into **Figma** to edit layers, paths, and text.

## Workflow

1. **Intake & Canvas Choice:**
   - Instagram Portrait Feed (4:5): `1080 x 1350 px` (Recommended for max feed real estate).
   - Instagram Square (1:1): `1080 x 1080 px`.
   - Instagram / TikTok Story (9:16): `1080 x 1920 px`.
2. **Compose Layout (HTML/CSS/SVG):**
   - Write HTML canvas container with strict dimension constraints.
   - Use Google Fonts (e.g. *Plus Jakarta Sans*, *Inter*, *Clash Display*, *Syne*).
   - Build background vector art directly in SVG.
   - Apply strict typography hierarchy and whitespace buffers.
3. **Render to PNG:**
   - Execute `python3 scripts/render_graphic.py <input.html> <output.png> [width] [height]`.
4. **Export Standalone SVG:**
   - Assemble pure `.svg` file for Figma interoperability.
5. **Verify & Review:**
   - Check negative space balance, font legibility, contrast (minimum 4.5:1), and edge padding.
